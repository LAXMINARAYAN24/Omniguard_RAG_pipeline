\section*{References}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{References}
\label{sec:references}

Bibliographic details below were checked directly against each paper's own
PDF (first-page title, author, and arXiv banner) rather than copied from an
earlier draft without verification; see the note following this list for
the one correction that check produced.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.2em,itemsep=0.5em]

\item Anonymous. \emph{Understanding Data Poisoning Attacks for RAG:
  Insights and Algorithms.} Under double-blind review as a conference
  paper at ICLR 2025. No arXiv identifier is printed on the submission
  itself, as expected for an anonymous review-track paper. Referred to
  throughout this report as \textbf{DRS}.

\item Haozhen Wang, Haoyue Liu, Jionghao Zhu, and Zhichao Wang.
  \emph{PIDP-Attack: Combining Prompt Injection with Database Poisoning
  Attacks on Retrieval-Augmented Generation Systems.} The Chinese University
  of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. arXiv:2603.25164, March 2026. Referred to
  throughout this report as \textbf{PIDP} or \textbf{PIDP-Attack}.

\item Longzhu He, Xiyu Zhang, Quan Liu, Chaozhuo Li, Zheng Liu, Pengpeng
  Zhou, and Sen Su. \emph{Push and Pull: Defending against Retrieval
  Poisoning Attacks via Embedding Space Reshaping.} Beijing University of
  Posts and Telecommunications; Beijing Academy of Artificial Intelligence;
  China Academy of Civil Aviation Science and Technology; Chongqing
  University of Posts and Telecommunications. ACM Transactions on
  Information Systems (TOIS), 2026. No arXiv identifier appears on the
  submission. Referred to throughout this report as \textbf{ShieldRAG} or
  \textbf{Push and Pull}.

\item Pushkal Kumar, Tucker Nielson, Tanish Kolhe, Shubham Zala, and Vincent
  Li. \emph{RAGuard: A Layered Defense Framework for Retrieval-Augmented
  Generation Systems Against Data Poisoning.} arXiv:2607.26339, July 2026.
  Referred to throughout this report as \textbf{RAGuard} or
  \textbf{RAGuard/ZKIP}.

\item Susil Kumar Mohanty, Rohit Patel, Kosuru Yuvaraj, Jeenal Chaudhary,
  and Disha Singhania. \emph{TriShieldRAG: 3 Rings, One Blind Spot in
  Layered Defenses for Retrieval-Augmented Generation.} Department of
  Computer Science and Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology
  Jodhpur. arXiv:2607.23838, July--August 2026. Referred to throughout this
  report as \textbf{TriShield} or \textbf{TriShieldRAG}.

\item Jiachen Qian. \emph{SilentRetrieval: Hijacking Retrieval-Augmented
  Generation via Semantically-Preserving Adversarial Data Poisoning.} City
  University of Hong Kong. arXiv:2605.28074, May 2026. ACM SIGKDD 2026.
  Referred to throughout this report as \textbf{SilentRetrieval}.

\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Correction made during report preparation.} An earlier internal
draft of this reference list gave DRS the arXiv identifier
\texttt{2603.25164} --- which is in fact PIDP-Attack's own identifier
(entry 2 above), evidently duplicated in error. Checking DRS's own PDF
directly confirms it is submitted under double-blind review with no visible
arXiv banner on the title page, so it has no arXiv identifier to cite at
all; the incorrect entry has been removed rather than left in place. The
same check added SilentRetrieval's real identifier (entry 6), which the
earlier draft had omitted.
